## Four adversarial anti-misread checks

1. **“Ready” is not execution authorization.** It means only that the listed
   non-executing artifact and its regression evidence are present.
2. **An approval packet is data, not a trigger.** `approve`, `ready`, or an Owner
   decision field cannot dispatch, claim, call OpenClaw, or issue a token.
3. **A rollback preview is text, not a Git action.** Validation success does not
   authorize `git revert`, file mutation, or an audit write.
4. **A rehearsal is not the Phase 9 real call.** Mock output, tmp-path board files,
   golden vectors, and a hypothetical READY report do not satisfy either Owner gate.

Phase 8 planning/projection and Phase 10 research remain outside the six v1.0
completion items. They cannot be counted as progress through Phase 7 or Phase 9.

## Conclusion

v1.0 remains unfinished at exactly two gates: Phase 7 local append-only audit
writing and Phase 9 Owner-present N=1 controlled execution. Everything in this
document is evidence or a blocker description; nothing here unlocks either gate.
